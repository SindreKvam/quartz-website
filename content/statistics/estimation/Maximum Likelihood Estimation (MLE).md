## Definition
$$
\hat{\theta}=\arg\max_{\theta}{p(\mathbf{x};\theta)}
$$
The practical procedure that is done to find the ML estimator is;
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ln p(\boldsymbol{x};\boldsymbol{\theta}) = 0
$$
___
### Properties
Properties of the ML estimator:
- If the MVU Estimator exists, then the ML estimator is the MVU estimator.
- Asymptotically Unbiased
- Asymptotically Efficient
- Asymptotically Gaussian
