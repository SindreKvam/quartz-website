## Motivation
The goal of detection, is to be able to decide when an event of interest occurs.
We would for example like to determine the presence of a signal within noise.
___
## Terminology
- ***Performance of detector***: A comparison of how often we detect an event, versus how often we receive false alarms. (Can be found using [[Receiver Operating Characteristics (ROC)|ROC]]).
___
## Detection Methods
- Likelihood ratio test (LRT) using [[Neyman-Pearson Theorem]].
- Finding the [[Test statistic|test statistic]] and characterising it.
- Finding [[Deterministic Signals|deterministic signals]].
